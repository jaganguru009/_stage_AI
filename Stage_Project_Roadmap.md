# Stage Project Roadmap

## Guiding approach

Stage should launch one city at a time and build supply-side demand first. Before broad artist acquisition, the launch city needs 30-50 active organizations posting real opportunities. The first product release should optimize for trusted professional identity and successful hiring, not social engagement or early revenue.

**Planning horizon:** 24 months from project kickoff. Timelines are indicative; progression is based on the exit criteria below.

| Phase | Indicative timing | Outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| 0. Foundation and validation | Months 0-2 | A launch city, pilot organizations, workflows, and MVP design are validated. |
| 1. Professional marketplace MVP | Months 3-6 | Organizations can find, assess, contact, and hire verified talent. |
| 2. Production operating system | Months 7-12 | Teams manage productions and build durable, credible work histories. |
| 3. Intelligence and ecosystem revenue | Months 13-18 | Matching, analytics, and adjacent services improve outcomes and retention. |
| 4. Category expansion | Months 19-24+ | The model expands to new cities and adjacent performing-arts markets. |

---

## Phase 0 - Foundation and validation (Months 0-2)

**Objective:** Prove the first-city wedge and design the smallest reliable marketplace workflow.

### Deliverables

- Select one launch city and theatre segment; define the initial geographic boundary.
- Recruit 5-10 design-partner organizations and identify community ambassadors.
- Conduct workflow interviews with directors, casting teams, production managers, and artists.
- Define verification policy for identity, credits, endorsements, and dispute handling.
- Map the core hiring journey: organization onboarding -> casting post -> discovery -> application/contact -> shortlist -> hire -> completed credit.
- Define the MVP information architecture, UX prototypes, data model, privacy policy, and moderation playbook.
- Establish baseline metrics and an analytics/event-tracking plan.

### Exit criteria

- At least 5 pilot organizations commit to testing the product.
- Clear evidence that existing hiring is fragmented across informal channels.
- A prioritized MVP backlog and a measurable definition of a successful hire.

---

## Phase 1 - Professional marketplace MVP (Months 3-6)

**Objective:** Make Stage the trusted place for organizations to discover, evaluate, and contact local performing-arts talent.

### Product scope

- Artist accounts and professional profiles: role/discipline, skills, location, availability, bio, media portfolio, and contact preferences.
- Organization profiles with administrator roles and verification status.
- Casting/opportunity creation, publication, application, shortlist, and status management.
- Search and filters for roles, skills, location, availability, and verified credits.
- Private in-product messaging with safety controls, reporting, and blocking.
- Basic profile and organization verification; manual review queue for the pilot.
- Production credit capture once a hiring outcome is confirmed.
- Product analytics dashboard for the Stage operations team.

### Go-to-market and operations

- Onboard organizations through ambassadors and founder-led sales before public artist marketing.
- Invite artists through the opportunities created by those organizations.
- Provide concierge onboarding for pilot organizations and high-potential artists.
- Run a weekly trust-and-safety review for identity, content, and reported interactions.

### Success metrics

- 30-50 active organizations in the launch city.
- At least 3 active opportunities per month per engaged organization segment.
- 60%+ of pilot organizations return to post or search again within 60 days.
- At least 25% of posted opportunities result in a shortlist or confirmed hire through Stage.
- Profile completion rate, time to shortlist, applications per opportunity, and message response rate tracked from day one.

### Exit criteria

- Organizations repeatedly use Stage for a real hiring workflow, not only profile browsing.
- A reliable manual verification process and clear user trust signals are in place.
- Marketplace liquidity is demonstrated in one city before expansion.

## Phase 2 - Production operating system and reputation (Months 7-12)

**Objective:** Turn every completed production into a trusted career asset and make Stage useful throughout the production lifecycle.

### Product scope

- Production workspaces: production details, team roster, roles, schedules, files, tasks, and announcements.
- Role-based collaboration for directors, assistant directors, production managers, cast, and crew.
- Verified production credits with organization confirmation and an artist review/dispute path.
- Professional endorsements tied to specific projects or competencies.
- Reputation signals that privilege verified work and reliable collaboration over follower counts.
- Organization and artist analytics: profile views, sourcing funnel, opportunity performance, and hiring outcomes.
- Improved search ranking based on role fit, availability, verified credits, and endorsements.

### Go-to-market and operations

- Convert pilot organizations to paid beta subscriptions only after repeat usage is established.
- Recruit ambassadors to validate profile quality and champion production-workspace adoption.
- Publish marketplace standards for credits, endorsements, and professional conduct.

### Success metrics

- 50%+ of hiring organizations create or complete at least one production workspace.
- 70%+ of completed productions generate verified credits.
- 50%+ of organizations return within 90 days.
- Organization subscription conversion is tested, while retention remains the primary decision metric.

### Exit criteria

- Verified credits and repeat production usage create a measurable advantage over informal channels.
- Retention and trust signals support expanding to a second city.

---

## Phase 3 - Intelligence and ecosystem revenue (Months 13-18)

**Objective:** Improve matching quality and deepen the value of the professional ecosystem.

### Product scope

- Explainable AI-assisted talent matching and role recommendations, with human review and opt-out controls.
- Talent and organization recommendations based on verified roles, skills, availability, credits, and prior collaboration.
- Advanced organization analytics: sourcing efficiency, role-fill time, talent-pool health, and production benchmarks.
- Premium artist membership experiments, featured listings, and verification-fee packaging.
- Workshops and education marketplace pilot.
- Ticketing pilot only where it integrates naturally with a production and does not distract from hiring/production core workflows.

### Success metrics

- AI recommendations improve shortlist-to-hire conversion against the baseline.
- Meaningful retention lift for organizations using analytics or recommendations.
- Revenue experiments demonstrate willingness to pay without harming marketplace participation.
- A second city reaches Phase 1 liquidity targets using the repeatable playbook.

### Exit criteria

- Matching is demonstrably fair, explainable, and better than filter-only discovery.
- At least one adjacent revenue stream shows repeatable unit economics.

---

## Phase 4 - Category and geographic expansion (Months 19-24+)

**Objective:** Reuse the verified-identity and production-history network in adjacent creative markets.

### Expansion sequence

1. Add additional theatre cities using the proven organization-first launch playbook.
2. Expand into regional cinema and OTT creators where credit verification and casting workflows transfer directly.
3. Extend to live events, festivals, and cultural organizations.
4. Evaluate international expansion only after regional operations, moderation, payment, and verification policies are repeatable.

### Product and platform investments

- Multi-city discovery, localization, regional verification partners, and scalable moderation.
- Enterprise analytics and organization administration.
- API/integration strategy for ticketing, event tools, and partner organizations where demand is proven.
- Data governance, consent management, audit trails, and performance/scalability work.

### Success metrics

- New cities reach marketplace-liquidity targets within a defined launch window.
- Expansion categories retain users and complete verified credits at rates comparable to theatre.
- Revenue per active organization grows without reducing repeat hiring behavior.

## Cross-phase dependencies and risks

| Dependency / risk | Roadmap response |
|---|---|
| Marketplace cold start | Onboard organizations first; invite artists through concrete opportunities. |
| Low trust in profiles | Make verification, confirmed credits, and project-specific endorsements core platform primitives. |
| Premature scope growth | Do not build ticketing, workshops, or broad AI features before the hiring loop is liquid and retained. |
| Reputation bias or gaming | Use evidence-linked signals, transparent policies, human review, and an appeals path. |
| Operational burden | Start verification and moderation manually; automate only after policy and edge cases are understood. |
| Weak geographic fit | Expand only after a city has repeat organization usage and completed verified productions. |

## Immediate next 30 days

1. Choose the launch city and theatre sub-segment.
2. Recruit the first 5-10 organization design partners and 2-3 ambassadors.
3. Validate the end-to-end hiring workflow through interviews and prototype tests.
4. Finalize MVP scope, trust policies, measurement plan, and delivery team plan.
